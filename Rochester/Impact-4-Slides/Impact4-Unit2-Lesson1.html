Unit 2 · It Takes a Village Part A
👥 Discuss with your base groups
Amish barn raising in Ohio, US
“By working together, we can make communities more visible.”— Ana Luísa Teixeira
  1. Look at the photo. Why do you think there are so many people working together?
  2. Read the quote. Why do you think visibility is important to a community?
  3. Think about the title, the quote, and the photo. What do you think “It Takes a Village” means in this context?
  4. When people are in need in your community, how can you help? Think of different problems that affect your community and ways you can take part in the solution.
