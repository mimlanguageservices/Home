Future Tenses Quiz
Teams of 3–4 · 3 rounds · 30 seconds per question · 1 point per correct answer
Future simple
will + verb
I will send it tomorrow.
Future continuous
will be + verb-ing
At 8 p.m. I will be working.
Future perfect
will have + past participle
By Friday I will have finished.
