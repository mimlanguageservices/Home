Case Study
MIM
The Hot Coffee Victim

In 1994, in the state of New Mexico, USA, a woman bought a cup of coffee at a restaurant. Outside of the restaurant, in a car, she had an accident — she dropped her coffee on herself. She burned herself.

The woman went to court and sued the restaurant because the coffee was hot.

What do you think happened next?

●
The judge decided that the restaurant had to pay her compensation.
●
The restaurant had to pay the woman USD $2,800,000.
